Why this portfolio is different
Most copy is written
for an imaginary customer

Most brands write one ad, one email, one page — and run it to everyone. Same copy. Completely different minds on the other end. Almost all of them wrong.

The problem is never the product. It’s that a cold stranger and a warm almost-buyer are reading identical words written for neither of them. One ignores it because nothing reflects his reality yet. The other ignores it because his one remaining objection was never addressed.

Great copy doesn’t just sound good. It meets the right person at the right psychological moment — in the ad that stops them, the VSL that shifts their belief, and the email that handles their objection before they voice it. Every asset in the funnel has a specific psychological job. This portfolio shows what that looks like in practice.

Stage 01
Pattern Interrupt Hook
Scrolling passively → emotionally present and personally identified
Strategic Purpose
The average viewer decides whether to keep watching within 3 seconds. No product mention. No solution. Just a mirror held up to his exact daily experience so uncomfortably specific that stopping feels impossible.
Script

I know how frustrating it is when you get a party invitation and think twice before confirming. You feel insecure standing next to physically fit friends — and deep down you wish they hadn’t invited you at all.

So you cancel at the last minute with a made-up excuse.

And then regret it that same night — watching everyone’s photos light up your WhatsApp.

Stage 02
Big Promise
“This is my painful reality” → “That life is actually possible for me”
Strategic Purpose
After 30 seconds of pain the prospect needs a glimpse of the exit. The destination shown through one specific scene — not features or claims. No product mention. Just the future made tangible and believable.
Script

Picture yourself at the front of the group photo — not because you practiced the pose, just because that’s how you feel. Confident. Present. Actually there.

And at the end of the night, you’re the one asking the photographer for every shot — so you can send them to the friends who cancelled. The ones who used to cancel like you once did.

Stage 03
Credibility Bridge
“Who is this person” → “This person understands exactly where I am”
Strategic Purpose
Trust cannot be claimed — it must be earned through shared experience. The coach is introduced not through credentials but through relevant personal struggle. Struggle first, results second — the only credibility sequence that works on cold traffic.
Script

Five years ago I was that guy. I cancelled a friend’s invitation, locked myself in my room — and stared at the ceiling wondering how I’d ended up here.

Then a rugby match came on TV. I don’t know why I kept watching. But something about those men — the way they moved, the way they carried themselves — I couldn’t look away. The next morning I found a local academy and walked in.

Six months later I attended my first party in years. I didn’t hide. I didn’t plan my exit before I arrived. I just... showed up.

After that I started helping the people closest to me — 20 school friends, 5 family members — all carrying the same weight I used to carry. They call me an angel now. I just call it knowing what actually works.

Stage 04
Problem Agitation
“This is socially uncomfortable” → “This is quietly destroying what matters most”
Strategic Purpose
Stage 1 showed surface pain. Stage 4 goes underneath to the intimate cost — marriage, connection, the silence that says more than words. Pain escalation is deliberate: surface pain makes him nod, deep pain makes him act. This stage makes inaction feel more expensive than £2,500.
Script

When you sit at dinner with your wife, you feel she’s not responding passionately. When it comes to fitness she loses the taste even if she doesn’t say it openly.

It’s costing her mental peace and honestly you know this. Even if you work hard, earn money, do your best to keep her happy… everything multiplies with zero when this one thing is missing.

Stage 05
Villain Reveal
“I keep failing because I’m the problem” → “I was given the wrong tools for my body”
Strategic Purpose
The biggest conversion killer at this stage is self-blame. A man who believes he is the problem also believes he’ll fail again — so why start? This stage removes that block permanently by naming the real enemy: biology working against an outdated approach. He didn’t fail the program. The program was built for a different body.
Script

This was never about effort or discipline.

You pushed through workouts but your shirt still didn’t fit after weeks of training.

After 30, testosterone drops. Your body recovers slower and burns fat slower than before. Your muscles stopped building despite the work.

The program you joined failed. Not you.

Stage 06
Mechanism
“Something different might work” → “I understand exactly why this specific approach will work for my body”
Strategic Purpose
Belief without logic doesn’t convert at £2,500. This stage gives the prospect a specific, named system explaining WHY this works when everything else failed. Named mechanisms create ownership, memorability, and logical buy-in simultaneously.
Script

For men over 35, your body takes 48 to 72 hours to repair what a 25-year-old does in 24 hours. But recovery is only one part — your body needs food that supports testosterone production naturally.

Most men keep failing because standard programs focus on training only but ignore recovery windows and age-specific nutrition. You need a training load matched to your hormonal state after 35.

That’s what I call The Age-Specific Training Reset.

You were not stuck… You were just following the instructions written for a 25-year-old body.

Stage 07
Proof
“This sounds logical” → “This has already worked for someone in my exact situation”
Strategic Purpose
Logic opens the mind. Story closes it. Not generic before/after claims — a named, detailed human story mirroring the prospect’s exact situation. The proof story answers his final skepticism question: “Has this worked for someone exactly like me?”
Script

Robert was 45, a CEO, and tried 2 different training programs in the last two years. His wife stopped saying anything about his body, which was somehow worse than criticism. And it was showing up at work too — he didn’t feel confident in his company’s meetings. He lost confidence and avoided eye contact which undermined his authority as a CEO.

One day he contacted me and from his tone of voice, I felt like this was his last attempt. Six weeks into The Age-Specific Training Reset, his shirts felt loose and he bought new ones.

Twelve weeks later he sent me a photo from his company meeting — standing at the head of the table and commanding the room.

Stage 08
Offer + CTA
“I believe this works” → “Booking this call is the obvious intelligent next step”
Strategic Purpose
The close doesn’t push — it pulls. Price anchored against the real cost of inaction already felt across seven stages. Soft exclusivity creates urgency without fake scarcity. One single next step removes decision friction entirely.
Script
